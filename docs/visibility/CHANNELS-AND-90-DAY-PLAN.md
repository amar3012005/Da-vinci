## Founder post drafts — LinkedIn (each requires approval)

### Post 1 — The definition

We’re building SINGULANCE around proactive intelligence: AI that notices what needs attention, connects it to your goals, plans the next useful step and carries approved work forward.

The practical challenge is continuity. What changed? What has already been done? What is the agent allowed to do next? And when does a person need to decide?

Our approach brings together HIVEMIND company context, Runtime coordination and specialist HyperAgents. It is early access, and we are testing the operating behavior as carefully as the individual capabilities.

Here is how we define the idea: https://singulancelabs.com/proactive-intelligence

### Post 2 — Initiative needs boundaries

A new email is a signal, not permission to do everything it contains.

For an agent working across company tools, initiative has to stay connected to goals and existing authority. Sometimes the next useful step is preparing a draft. Sometimes it is asking one necessary question. Sometimes it is quietly deciding that nothing needs doing.

That distinction is central to what we are building at SINGULANCE.

https://singulancelabs.com/proactive-intelligence

### Post 3 — A deliverable is not always an outcome

A research report can support a decision. It does not prove an email was sent, a meeting booked or a customer conversation completed.

One of the operating rules we are working on at SINGULANCE is to verify the actual result of the assignment, then connect it to the next dependency. Useful agent work needs that distinction between producing something and moving the agenda forward.

https://singulancelabs.com/proactive-intelligence

### Post 4 — Memory should survive the conversation

A long chat history is not the same as usable working context.

An agent needs to carry forward decisions, evidence, completed actions and unresolved dependencies without rediscovering all of them every time a session resumes. That is one reason memory research matters to SINGULANCE's product direction.

ICARUS explores agent memory; Runtime explores continuity of company work. The methods and limits belong alongside the ambition.

https://singulancelabs.com/research/icarus
