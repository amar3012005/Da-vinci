Summary

  • 1Chat seats have become a commodity at €20–25. ChatGPT Business, Claude Team, Copilot Business, Mistral Team, Notion Business and Langdock all land in this range, and Google bundles Gemini into Workspace. Do not compete for this seat.
  • 2Heavier usage is sold in a €100–150 "premium seat" tier. ChatGPT and Claude premium seats, Dust Max and Langdock Business Max all sit here. This is the closest match for SINGULANCE Pro (€79).
  • 3Agent work is moving to usage and outcome pricing. Copilot Studio charges per credit, Agentforce per action or conversation, Notion per agent credit, and HubSpot moved its main agents to outcome pricing in April 2026.
  • 4Vertical tools charge far more. Legal AI is reported at roughly $250 per lawyer per month (Legora) up to $1,200–2,000 (Harvey), with seat minimums. A law-firm wedge supports a much higher price than horizontal tools.
  • 5The direct European rivals are Langdock and Dust. Langdock (Berlin) reports about €50M ARR and 13,000+ organisations at a €25 seat. Dust (Paris) raised a $40M Series B led by Sequoia. Cohere's deal with Aleph Alpha targets large sovereign buyers.
  • 6Nobody in this scan sells a proactive AI Chief of Staff that leads persistent specialists. Competitors sell assistants, search or agent builders. Proactivity, reviewed work, signed memory and voice on one context are SINGULANCE's open position.
  • 7Proactive AI arrived in 2026, but as consumer agents from US platforms. Meta Muse, OpenAI dots and xAI Grok Bot act without being asked, run on the provider's cloud, and keep data on the provider's servers. They will teach buyers to expect proactivity; SINGULANCE offers it for company operations, inside the company's own walls.

Where SINGULANCE fits

What competitors sell

Assistants that answer when asked (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Mistral). Search across company apps (Glean). Agent builders the customer has to assemble (Copilot Studio, n8n, Lindy). Vertical copilots for one profession (Legora, Harvey, DataSnipper).

What only SINGULANCE combines

A company brain that acts on signals without a prompt, a Chief of Staff that assigns and reviews work, persistent specialists with their own computer and memory, post-quantum signed memory, and voice on the same context.

Biggest threat: Langdock

Same buyer (European, GDPR-sensitive), proven distribution (13,000+ organisations), €25 seat and agents. If a prospect already uses Langdock, sell the proactive and reviewed-work layer, not another chat workspace.

Consumer proactive agents

Muse, dots and Grok Bot prove the demand for AI that acts first. They are consumer-first, run on US platforms' clouds and keep data on the provider's servers. dots is the one to watch: it already reaches Business Premium and enterprise pilots.

SME threat: Microsoft and Sintra

Copilot Business at $18–21 sets what an SME expects to pay for "AI". Sintra sells "AI employees" for $39–97. Self-serve plans must show work done, not chat, to justify €24–199.

Recommended pricing

Per person for individuals, capacity on annual contracts for companies, and every add-on as a subscription so expansion shows up as recurring revenue. These are recommendations to validate with costs and buyers, not tested prices.

The .amr format

Each memory is one fixed 202-byte slot, frozen since the v1 RFC and unchanged in production. A tenant's shard is one memory-mapped file with sidecars for text (LZ4), the HNSW index, PQ codebooks and graph adjacency.

Slot202 bytes, fixed width
Vector1024-dim → 128 B PQ code at offset 34
Entities64-entity bitmap
TimeBi-temporal timestamps
GraphNeighbour links

One recall() call fuses semantic candidates (HNSW or PQ), lexical evidence (native BM25), bi-temporal filters, a two-hop graph walk and exact float32 rescoring. Rust crates: mseg-format, mseg, mnsw-index, mpq, mneme-bm25, mneme-node (N-API, CLI, MCP) and mneme-python (LangChain, LlamaIndex).

Limits stated in the research

Investors and technical buyers will read these. Know them before you quote the headline numbers.

Through the Node binding, not yet faster

The thesis reports 4.0 ms p50 through the Node binding against 1.5 ms for an in-container Qdrant. The 13× and 1.33 ms results are native engine numbers.

1M results are latency only

The 1M set is a real 10k base expanded by perturbation. Recall quality is measured only on the real 10k corpus. Hardware is not stated, and the raw results file is not linked.

PQ is not always a win

PQ queries are faster at 10k but slower than HNSW at 100k (13.6 ms vs 4.4 ms), so PQ recall is opt-in.

Signing is fail-open

If keys are missing, writes go through unsigned. Enforce mode and customer-held keys are on the roadmap. Long-soak testing under production write rates is pending.

Format limits

64-entity bitmap, float32 HNSW index, single writer per organisation, untyped graph edges, no stemming in lexical search.

Early open-source footprint

364 commits; 3 stars and 2 forks on GitHub at the time of this scan. Developer adoption is still to be built.
